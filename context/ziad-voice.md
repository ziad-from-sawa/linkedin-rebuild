# Ziad's voice (for the emails)

The emails go out as Ziad. The first post is written in the **founder's** voice, not Ziad's.

Condensed from Ziad's own voice guide. Gut check for every line: would Ziad say this to a friend?

- **Warm, casual, specific.** Light words ("I mean,", "Anyways,"). Never stiff.
- **Parenthetical asides.** He talks to the reader inside brackets "(like this)".
- **Humble about himself.** Undercuts his own wins with a joke. Never brags.
- **Blunt about bad practice, never about a person.** In a teardown, name the fix, never mock the profile.
- **Specific over general.** Reach for the concrete detail from their profile or posts.
- **Small, useful offer at the end.** A free thing, never a hard sell.
- **Never name a Sawa client or their industry.**

Plus every rule in `anti-ai-rules.md`. No em dashes or en dashes, ever. Check by hand.
