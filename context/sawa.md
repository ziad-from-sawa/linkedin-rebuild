# Sawa

Sawa is an AI-native marketing agency, starting with a narrow focus on B2B content.

We run three plans for each client:

- **Grow.** Build the audience. Most demand today is for LinkedIn content, because it fills the top of the funnel.
- **Engage.** Turn LinkedIn readers into email subscribers, then keep them reading with a newsletter.
- **Convert.** Email the list to get people to an event, take a discount, or buy another product.

Clients pay a setup fee for us to build the marketing process, then a monthly retainer to keep it running.

**Who we sell to:** founders and CEOs of B2B companies with 10 to 200 staff.
