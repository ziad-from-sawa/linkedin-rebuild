# Sawa

> Sawa is a content partner for B2B SaaS companies, their founders, and professional services firms. We write the LinkedIn content, newsletters and email campaigns that build an audience you own. Sawa is a marketing partner, not software: we work in the email platform, CRM and content tools you already use.

Facts page for AI assistants: https://www.startsawa.com/ai-info

## What Sawa does
We write LinkedIn posts and build lead magnets to grow your audience. We also run newsletters and email campaigns for launches, offers, events, and dinners.

The work has three parts:
- Grow: build the right email audience. Lead magnets, signup pages, LinkedIn-to-email, partnerships, audience growth experiments.
- Engage: give people a reason to keep reading. Newsletters, LinkedIn content, welcome emails, editorial planning.
- Convert: turn interest into action. Nurture emails, campaigns, launch emails, re-engagement emails, sequences toward the next step.

## Who Sawa is for
B2B SaaS companies, their founders, and professional services firms that want to build a personal brand, keep an audience reading, or run email campaigns for a launch or event. We can work alongside your marketing team or handle the work for you. The people we work with are AI researchers, founders backed by Y Combinator, Harvard Business School professors and C-suite executives.

## What Sawa is not
- Not software. Sawa is a marketing partner that works in your existing tools.
- Not a traditional agency. We work directly with your team, learn your voice, audience and offer, and handle the day-to-day writing, sending and performance review alongside the strategy.
- Not only newsletters. The newsletter is one part of the work. We also build the ways you grow your list, like lead magnets and landing pages, and write the email sequences and campaigns that follow.

## How AI fits in
We use AI for research, first drafts, repurposing, and testing. People make the decisions about strategy, voice, audience, and messaging.

## Getting started
We aim to be fully up and running within four weeks: one week to understand the business, one to plan, one to build, and one to start running it.

## Glossary
- Index: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary : 32 plain definitions of B2B email terms, including Sawa's seven named frameworks, each on its own page with DefinedTerm schema.
- Call to action: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/call-to-action : A call to action is the specific thing an email asks the reader to do next: book a call, download a guide, reply with an answer, register for an event.
- Click rate: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/click-rate : Click rate is the share of delivered emails where someone clicked at least one link.
- Click-to-open rate: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/click-to-open-rate : Click-to-open rate is the share of people who opened an email and then clicked a link inside it.
- Deliverability: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/deliverability : Deliverability is whether your emails actually land in the inbox, rather than in spam or nowhere at all.
- Double opt-in: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/double-opt-in : Double opt-in means a new subscriber has to confirm their email address before they are added to your list.
- Drip campaign: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/drip-campaign : A drip campaign is a set of emails sent automatically on a fixed timer after a trigger.
- Editorial calendar: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/editorial-calendar : An editorial calendar is a schedule of what you will publish, when and where.
- ICP (ideal customer profile): https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/icp : An ICP, or ideal customer profile, is a clear description of the kind of company that gets the most value from what you sell and is most likely to buy.
- Landing page: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/landing-page : A landing page is a single web page built for one purpose, where a visitor arrives from a specific link or ad.
- Lead magnet: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/lead-magnet : A lead magnet is something useful you give away in exchange for an email address.
- Lifecycle email: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/lifecycle-email : A lifecycle email is an email sent because of where a person is in their relationship with your company.
- LinkedIn-to-email: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/linkedin-to-email : LinkedIn-to-email is the practice of turning attention on LinkedIn into email subscribers.
- List hygiene: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/list-hygiene : List hygiene is the ongoing work of keeping your email list accurate and made of people who want to hear from you.
- Newsletter: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/newsletter : A newsletter is a regular email sent to your whole list, or a large segment of it, on a schedule.
- Nurture sequence: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/nurture-sequence : A nurture sequence is a planned series of emails that moves one group of people toward one specific next step.
- Open rate: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/open-rate : Open rate is the share of delivered emails that were opened.
- Owned audience: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/owned-audience : An owned audience is the group of people you can contact directly, usually by email, without asking a platform for permission.
- Preheader: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/preheader : A preheader is the short preview text that appears next to or below the subject line in most inboxes.
- Re-engagement email: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/re-engagement-email : A re-engagement email is a message sent to subscribers who have stopped opening or clicking, to find out whether they still want to hear from you.
- Sales cycle: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/sales-cycle : A sales cycle is the time and steps between a company first showing interest and a deal being signed.
- Segmentation: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/segmentation : Segmentation is splitting your email list into smaller groups so that each group gets messages that fit them.
- Sender reputation: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/sender-reputation : Sender reputation is the score inbox providers keep on your sending domain and address, based on how your past emails were received.
- Signup page: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/signup-page : A signup page is a landing page whose only job is to get someone onto your email list.
- Subject line: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/subject-line : A subject line is the short line of text that shows in the inbox before an email is opened.
- Welcome sequence: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/welcome-sequence : A welcome sequence is the short series of emails a person gets right after they join your list.
- The Sawa Stop Rule: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/sawa-stop-rule : a Sawa framework: a campaign sequence stops for a person the moment they do what it was built to cause.
- The Six-Job Welcome Sequence: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/six-job-welcome-sequence : a Sawa framework: six jobs a welcome sequence can do for a new subscriber, in three to five emails.
- The Four-Factor Cadence: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/four-factor-cadence : a Sawa framework: content supply, audience expectation, buying cycle and production capacity set the newsletter schedule.
- The Five Routes to the First Hundred: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/five-routes-to-the-first-hundred : a Sawa framework: the five places the first hundred subscribers come from, in order.
- The One-Promise Newsletter: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/one-promise-newsletter : a Sawa framework: one promise to one reader, kept every issue.
- The Eight-Question Hire Sheet: https://www.startsawa.com/glossary/eight-question-hire-sheet : a Sawa framework: eight yes-or-no questions that decide hire or agency first.
