01 / The workflow

LinkedIn Rebuild

Paste a B2B founder's LinkedIn URL. Get back a teardown of their profile, a rebuilt version of it, a first post and two emails for Ziad to send.

Ziad Hassan · founder, PM Charlotte Driscoll Yuri Mukai Dan Chauvin
1 · Startup

Sawa

An AI-native marketing agency, starting with a narrow focus on B2B content. Each client gets three plans:

  • Grow. LinkedIn content fills the top of the funnel.
  • Engage. Readers become newsletter subscribers.
  • Convert. Emails that get them to an event, a discount or an upsell.

Clients pay a setup fee, then a monthly retainer to keep it running.

2 · Why now

LinkedIn is where the demand is

Our biggest demand is LinkedIn content. The best pitch to a founder is their own profile, rebuilt, before we ever talk.

Ziad wants every pitch built from the founder's own profile and posts. This workflow makes one from a single URL.

3 · User and trigger

User: Ziad. He runs it and approves everything before it goes out.

Trigger: a founder Ziad wants as a client. He pastes their URL.

4 · Input

One LinkedIn profile URL.

Real example: linkedin.com/in/ziad-has, Ziad's own profile and our model of a good one.

Target: founders and CEOs of B2B companies, 10 to 200 staff.

5 · Steps
1PullApifyProfile plus the last 90 days of posts, saved as JSON.
2ScoreClaude CodePosting stats and Ziad's 7-point checklist. Each check is Pass or Fix, with the fact behind it.
3RebuildClaude CodeHTML mockup of the top of their profile, fixed. Colors from their company site.
4PostClaude CodeOne first post we would write for them, in their voice, from their own material.
5EmailClaude CodeEmail 1 shows the work, with no link. Email 2 is a bump with the 15-minute booking link.
6ReviewZiadReads the whole folder. Drafts the emails into his own inbox.
6 · Output

One folder per founder, out/<name>/:

  • teardown.md
  • mockup.html
  • first-post.md
  • email-1.md
  • email-2-bump.md

Booking link (email 2 only): calendly.com/ziad-startsawa/15min

What great looks like

Ziad's checklist for a good founder profile

  1. Headshot on a solid color background
  2. Website link under the name
  3. A banner
  4. About section filled out, in bullets
  5. At least one featured post with an image
  6. Short, clear headline
  7. Follow button instead of Connect

Plus posting stats: posts in the last 90 days, days since the last post, average reactions and comments.

How we judge it
  • Every number matches the scraped data.
  • The email names one specific thing from their profile or posts.
  • Email 1 has no link.
  • Zero em dashes. Passes the anti-AI rules.
  • Ziad would send it without edits.
7 · Human checkpoints
  • Ziad reads the whole folder before anything leaves.
  • Emails land as drafts in Ziad's inbox. He sends them.
  • The bump goes only if there is no reply, and only when Ziad says so.
  • Nothing sends on its own.
8 · Definition of done

/rebuild <url> in Claude Code writes all five files.

Works on 3 real founder URLs with consistent results.

9 · Stretch goals
  • A web page with a URL box and a Generate button
  • Banner saved as a PNG at 1584 x 396
  • New headshot on a solid background
  • Emails dropped straight into Ziad's drafts
  • A list of URLs in, one folder each
10 · Workstreams
  • A · Data. Apify setup, the pull, the teardown. Owner brings their own Apify key.
  • B · Rebuild. The mockup and the first post.
  • C · Email and demo. Both emails, 3 test runs, the video.
  • Ziad. Answers questions and judges the output, without taking the keyboard.
11 · Inputs ready to use

Private repo: github.com/ziad-from-sawa/linkedin-rebuild. It holds this brief, the Sawa context, the checklist, the anti-AI rules, Ziad's voice notes and the test URLs. Open it in Claude Code and it reads CLAUDE.md first.

  • Who takes A, B and C?

  • Email 1: attach the mockup, or offer to send it?

02 / Build it

Starter checklist

Build each step alone and test it on Ziad's URL before adding the next. Rotate the keyboard. The teammate with the least AI experience drives first.

00Setup

Everyone · 10 min
  • Accept the GitHub invite. Clone linkedin-rebuild.
  • Open the folder in Claude Code: claude
  • Ask: "Read CLAUDE.md and the brief. Restate the workflow as numbered steps with inputs and outputs."
  • Fix anything Claude gets wrong before building.
Done when Claude restates all 6 steps correctly.

01Pull the data

Workstream A
  • Make a free Apify account. Paste your token into .env as APIFY_API_TOKEN.
  • Profile: apimaestro/linkedin-profile-batch-scraper-no-cookies-required
  • Posts: apimaestro/linkedin-batch-profile-posts-scraper. It has no date filter, so keep the last 90 days yourself.
  • Skip harvestapi actors. Free plans cap them at 10 runs.
  • Save raw JSON to out/<name>/raw/. Then score it against context/profile-checklist.md.
Done when teardown.md for Ziad's URL shows all 7 checks and every number matches the JSON.

02Produce the mockup

Workstream B
  • Build mockup.html: the top of their LinkedIn profile, with every Fix from the teardown fixed.
  • Include: banner, headshot on a solid color, short headline, website link, Follow button, About in bullets, one featured post with an image.
  • Pull their brand colors from their company website.
  • Open it in a browser and compare it to Ziad's profile.
Done when the mockup passes all 7 checks by eye.

03Produce the first post

Workstream B
  • Read their recent posts and About section first.
  • Write one post on a topic they already talk about, in their voice.
  • Use only facts from their profile or posts. Invent nothing.
  • Run the anti-AI rules. Search for dashes by hand.
Done when the founder could post it without explaining where a claim came from.

04Produce the email

Workstream C
  • Email 1: from Ziad, in his voice (context/ziad-voice.md). Opens with one specific detail from their profile. Shows the work. No links.
  • Email 2, the bump: short. Adds calendly.com/ziad-startsawa/15min for a 15-minute call.
  • The team writes the copy. There is no past winner to copy from.
  • Save both as drafts for Ziad. Never send.
Done when Ziad reads both and would send them as written.

05Save it as one command

A and C
  • Create .claude/commands/rebuild.md so /rebuild <url> runs steps 01 to 04 in order.
  • Run it on 3 founder URLs from inputs/test-urls.md.
  • Log what breaks. Fix one thing at a time.
Done when 3 runs give 3 complete folders of the same quality.

06Optional: UI/UX wrapper

Whoever finishes first
  • A local web page with one URL box and a Generate button. It runs claude -p "/rebuild <url>" and shows the five outputs side by side, with the mockup rendered.
